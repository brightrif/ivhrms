How to read this sheet
Type 8 for normal hours. For a worker on several projects, type 8+2 for normal plus overtime. To fill many boxes at once, drag across them (or Shift+click the last one), then type the hours: every selected box gets them. Press Enter to finish, Esc to cancel. Every working day the worker is on this site is open: saving hours on a day with no attendance marks it present. A day marked Absent or Leave stays closed. The grey number in an empty box is the hours that day expects, a dashed box is a present day with no hours yet, and a red box is a short day (fewer hours than expected, across all projects): check it against the paper. The Not entered chip counts working days with neither attendance nor hours. Grey columns are weekly offs and holidays: type the hours only. A dash means no attendance, and a letter means that day cannot take hours. Confirmed hours are locked.
