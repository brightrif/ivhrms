Clearance checklist

  • Cancel the work permit with the LMRA and the residence permit.

  • Return of company assets, keys, card and vehicle.
  • Return ticket to the home country, if the contract provides one.
  • Pay within the period the law allows, and give the signed statement.
  • Issue the end-of-service certificate (available once approved).
